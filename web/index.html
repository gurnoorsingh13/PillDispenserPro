AI assistant

Scan prescriptions, ask questions in any language, and get summaries. Uses Groq's free AI service.

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign in. It's free.
  2. Tap Create API key, copy it, paste it below and save.
  3. Tap Test.

What it does

Reads prescriptionsSchedule → Scan a prescription. You check before saving.
Answers questionsOn the Today page, or just message the Telegram bot: "Did Papa take his pills today?", "Dawai kab khatam hogi?"
Writes summariesHistory → AI summary.
Models
Groq sometimes retires models. Tap Test to see which ones your key can use.

Photos and questions are sent to Groq to be answered. The AI can make mistakes, so always check against the prescription and ask a doctor about medical questions.
